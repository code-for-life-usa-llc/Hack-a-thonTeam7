How do these wars keep occurring?

Wars start when nations or factions perceive that their political, economic, or territorial goals cannot be achieved through diplomacy, leading them to use armed conflict. This escalation is typically driven by a combination of competing interests, mutual mistrust, fear of attack, and the mobilization of resources and military forces. Wars may also start because of religious issues, ideological issues or maybe even revenge against a nation one might've had an issue with before. They can also occur when a country is divided by two different beliefs (a civil war).
